000007From the president

More than a student organization.

At Quinnipiac Blockchain, we are a collective of innovators, problem-solvers, and forward-thinkers. Our mission is to build a vibrant blockchain ecosystem at Quinnipiac by providing education, resources, and opportunities to explore this transformative technology — whether you're a seasoned enthusiast or just starting out.

Blockchain is not just about cryptocurrency. From finance and healthcare to supply chains and digital identity, it is designed to drive transparency, security, and efficiency. Our vision is to equip the next generation of blockchain leaders with the knowledge, skills, and industry connections to make a real impact.

— Marcus Opperman '27, President
